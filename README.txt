DIGITALGROW DEMO

How to run:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome/Edge.
3. Create a demo account.
4. Your referral code/link will appear in the dashboard.

Important:
This is a front-end demo. Data is stored only in your browser's localStorage.
It does NOT process real payments or real withdrawals.

Recommended real business model:
Commission should be generated from genuine digital-marketing services purchased by customers. Avoid making payment for recruitment itself the basis of earnings. Before launching publicly, have the business/payment/referral structure reviewed for applicable Pakistani legal and regulatory requirements.
